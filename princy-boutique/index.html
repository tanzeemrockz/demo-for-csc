Bespoke Journey

From Saree to Masterpiece

How our Thillai Nagar artisans craft your bridal blouse with millimeter precision.

1

Design & Saree Analysis

Bring your saree or send photos. We inspect the zari tone (antique, matte gold, silver) and discuss neckline depths.

2

Tracing & Motif Sketching

Our designers trace custom motifs suited to your body proportions. You approve the tracing paper stencil before needle touches silk.

3

Frame Aari Needlework

Stretched tightly across wooden cot frames, skilled artisans hand-thread French zardosi, cut beads, and pearls.

4

Cut & Trial Fitting

Precision master tailoring with premium padded cups and armhole shaping, finished with an in-store trial in Thillai Nagar.
