Commercial Inquiries

Frequently asked questions regarding orders, MOQ, and sampling.

What is the standard minimum order quantity for custom carton boxes?

Short-run trial batches for custom printed and die-cut duplex cartons start at 500 to 1,000 units. Volume price breaks apply on production runs of 5,000 units and above.

Can we inspect board GSM and lamination samples prior to production?

Yes. Clients are welcome to visit our facility opposite Koodal Alagar Perumal Kovil on West Vadampokki Street during regular hours to check material stock and finishing swatches.

What settlement methods are supported for institutional accounts?

We support direct RTGS/NEFT transfers, official company cheques, UPI payments, and cash settlements. GST invoices are issued for all business orders.
